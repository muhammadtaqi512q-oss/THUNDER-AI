Hackers Nuqsande Kyun Hain?

  • Mali Nuqsan (Financial Loss): Har aik attack par aam sarif ya idaray ka bhari mali nuqsan hota hai.
  • Data Ki Chori: Aap ki personal photos, passwords, aur bank details leak ho sakti hain.
  • Privacy Khatam: Aap ki ijazat ke bgher aap ke system par control hasil kiya jata hai.
  • Zehni Azziyat: Cyber attack ke baad victim shadeed zehni tanawo (stress) ka shikaar ho jata hai.

Hum Kis Level Tak Maaf Kar Sakte Hain?

Wazahat: Shuruati seekhne ke marahil (Learning Phase) mein students se ghaltiyan hoti hain. Magar cyber crimes mein aik had muqarrar karna zaroori hai:

  • Level 1 se Level 2: Mukammal tor par maafi di ja sakti hai kyun ke yeh nadani ya seekhne ka doran ho sakta hai.
  • Level 3 (Akhri Had): Yeh **Strict Maximum Limit** hai. Level 3 par total loss 6 Lakh tak pohnch jata hai. Yahan tak aakhri warning ke sath maaf kiya ja sakta hai.
  • Level 4 Aur Uss Se Agay (No Forgiveness): Level 4 par pohnchte hi hacker professionally nuqsan pohnchana shuru kar deta hai. Iss ke baad koi maafi nahi di ja sakti aur strict legal action/blacklistment zaroori ho jati hai.
